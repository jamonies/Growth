Personal Thoughts

What do I think of our current economic landscape? How do I interpret Economics? In its simplest form, it's the study of how societies allocate scarce resources. If you want to individualize things eve more so than that, you could say that economics is the study of human decision making under scarcity. Say that I wish to purchase an item for $10 and I currently have $15. I clearly possess more than enough to cover this cost (not accounting for sales tax in this exercise), but I also have been told that item is on sale for 'buy one, get one half off'. I either could stick to my original plan of purchasing one unit of said item, or I can pay for two. Is it worth it to have more than one? What if I need that extra $5 I would otherwise lose later if I purchased two but needed money for something unexepcted. That simple thought process is simple, but it is an economic one. If it's hard to decide what to do for a matter of that scale, imagine the challenges that arise when you have to make a much larger, broader choice. Or to raise the stakes even higher: having to make that choice as a leader on behalf of others who entrust their representation to you.
